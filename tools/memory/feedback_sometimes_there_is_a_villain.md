**Author, 2026-11-12 (Ch 5 decision walk):** "sometimes there is no villain. However… sometimes there very
much is a specific entity that is rent seeking and doing everything they can to shift the cost onto people
that have no standing to sit at the negotiation table, and no ability to refuse the burden of the cost."

**Why it matters.** Briefs, maps and rigor passes since May have carried "no-villain throughout" as a register
rule. Applied mechanically it softens documented conduct (concealed contamination, pleaded felonies, lobbying
on the record) into structure, which is less accurate and less honest.

**How to apply.** Keep "the mechanism does not need a villain" as the general reading. Where the record shows
a named party seeking rent or shifting a cost onto people who could not refuse, say so plainly and cite the
record. Never allege intent the record does not show.

**Same session, also the author's:** the whole book is told from his vantage; "you" is only for what any
reader anywhere has, never a nationality or country; "we" is author plus reader, or a named group he belongs
to; countries are named. "Alibi" is not his word and is retired in favor of "distance." Current statement:
`tools/drafting-templates/commons_bonds_house-style-sheet_v1.0.1.md`.
