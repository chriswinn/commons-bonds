# Google Drive: one exception to "never write," for the private Sources folder

**From:** the author, 2026-11-24, in the Chapter 5 session.
**Scope:** every session that handles source PDFs.

**The standing rule and why it exists.** Sessions do not write to Google Drive. The rule dates from a
text-truncation error in Drive writes that was tracked down earlier; it is why the project moved its working
files to GitHub.

**The exception (his decision).** Sessions may **upload and download PDF files** to and from **one private
folder in his Drive that holds the source PDFs he has obtained** (journal articles and book pages from his
library). Nothing else changes.

**What the exception does not cover.**
- No writing, editing or creating of Docs, Sheets, text or any other file type in Drive.
- No renaming, moving or deleting of anything already in Drive. Migration stays read-only on the Drive side.
- Never touch the `/Noema/` or `/Berggruen Institute - Essay/` folders.
- No sharing or permission changes on the folder or its files. It stays private to him.
- The PDFs still do not go into this repository. `research/citations/source-files-index.md` is the record of
  what each file is; update it in the same session as any upload.

**How to apply.**
- Use the folder only once he has connected Drive and named it; record its name in the index.
- Upload under the "Suggested file name" in the index. After an upload, check the file's size against the
  local copy and, where the tool allows, download it again and compare the SHA-256 fingerprint in the index.
  The truncation history is the reason for this check; a PDF that comes back changed is reported to him and
  not relied on.
- If the folder is not connected or a write is refused, say so and ask; do not look for another place to put
  the files.

*His words: "let's use Google Drive but only use it for this … allow you to upload and download .pdf files to
google drive for this usecase." The "how to apply" list is the session's reading.*

**Note for him:** his personal settings still say "Never write to Google Drive." Until that line carries this
exception, a new session may decline. Suggested wording: "Never write to Google Drive, except to upload or
download source PDFs in my private Sources folder (see tools/memory/feedback_drive_sources_folder_exception.md)."
