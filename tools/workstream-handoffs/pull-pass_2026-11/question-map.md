# Ch 5 rings: question map (made before drafting, 2026-11-10)

Internal. One page. The draft is `ch5-rings_2026-11-10.md` in this folder.

**The chapter's question.** No one in Butler's father's room chose to send the cost to her mother. So how
did it get there, and is a hospital room the only place it lands? Opened at the end of "The family."
Answered in two halves: *how* in "In your pocket, and in mine" (the same arrangement runs through every
ring, and through us), *what it is* in "The pattern, named" (a severed cost). The chapter's last line hands
the next question on: what would it take to post the bond before the cost is severed?

**Book questions advanced.** "Who is left holding the bill?" gets a new answer in every ring. "Is this the
full accounting?" is set up by the pocket (the reader gains as well as pays) and by the close (the reader's
own descendants).

**The term handed over:** "severed cost," in "The pattern, named," right after the reader has seen it in
every ring and in their own pocket.

**Scenes and bridges.** One opening scene (Butler's room, in her words). One earned return (the close, Q3:
the pacemaker still pulsing). Bridges run through relationships: the mother, a family asking its
community, the miners' children, the fishing families, parents and children in Social Security, our
descendants. One object bridge: the phone.
