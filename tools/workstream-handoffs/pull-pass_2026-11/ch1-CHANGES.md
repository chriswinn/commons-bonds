## Strongest-critic read (one read)

The critic is a hostile but fair reader: the same editor on the train, reading memoir as the door into a
policy book.

1. **The middle sags in the family section (about 1,900 words).** The grandfather, the compressor, the
   father's sayings, the chore, the hunting mornings, the room-holding and the nephew run back to back

   call, and yours.

   book keeps "Is this the full accounting?" as its running test.
4. **What the critic could not break:** the lease arithmetic, the plane returning to the colleague's
