    the hook. It needs only the author redline + band-trim + a news-peg activation to submit.
- **Prior research exists, parked as Book 2.** `archive/_OneDayMaybe/book-two/notes/counter-vance-research-notes.html`
  (April 2026) maps Vance's nine *Hillbilly Elegy* arguments for a structural counter-narrative and
  notes the material "could also feed Commons Bonds Chapter 5." It was deliberately filed as a
  **separate Book-2 project**, not Book 1. It pre-dates the project's fact-check discipline, so none
  of it is publisher-ready without a Pass-3.1-style fact-check first.
- **⚠ The real strategic tension (this is the author's decision).** A *named* "counter to JD Vance"
  piece is **partisan-coded** in a way the rest of the portfolio has been engineered NOT to be. The
  placement strategy rests on the cross-aisle, structure-not-blame stance: the $100 Barrel / Atlantic
  Ideas non-partisan framing; the Public Choice / rent-seeking "invitation to AEI, Cato, Mercatus,
  GMU" in Ch 5; and above all the **Darity academic-credibility route**, which is the current top
  priority and the most credible path to a press. Naming Vance as the antagonist trades that stance
  for topical heat. The success-criteria anchor ("routed through vocabulary adoption"; "open eyes and
  honest accounting"; *"when in doubt, trim"*) leans toward protecting the non-partisan position —
  especially while the Darity package is in flight and he's reading the samples to judge the writing.
- **The low-risk way to get most of the upside:** activate the **Guardian McDowell op-ed** with a
  light news-peg lede that gestures at the national culture-vs-structure debate **without making
  Vance the subject** — refute the *frame*, not the *man*. That rides the timeliness, keeps the
  structural high ground and the cross-aisle credibility, and uses an asset that's already drafted
  and ledger-clean. It needs only the author's redline + band-trim (2 micro-calls already open).
- **Named-subject discipline (if a Vance-named piece is ever pursued):** Vance is a living public
  figure. His *published* writing (*Hillbilly Elegy*) and *on-record* statements are citable
  (public-record exception); characterizations of his personal views/motives beyond the on-record
  text are not — refute the argument from the text, invent nothing, attribute nothing beyond it.
- **Window + verdict:** **decide at winter break, default = don't name Vance in a Book-1 piece.**
  Recommended path: (1) near-term, if you want to ride the Appalachia timeliness, activate the
  Guardian op-ed's news peg (frame, not man); (2) keep the explicit counter-Vance treatment as the
  Book-2 project it already is. Promote to a named standalone piece only on an explicit author "go,"
  with the partisan-positioning cost weighed against the Darity/academic strategy.
