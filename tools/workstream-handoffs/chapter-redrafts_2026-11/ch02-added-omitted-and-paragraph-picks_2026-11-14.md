# Chapter 2: what was added, what was omitted, and which version is best paragraph by paragraph (2026-11-14)

**Status:** PROPOSED (internal). Written at the author's request in Session 1. No draft was changed: every addition and omission stays in B and C as written, and this file only notes them and recommends.

**Caution about the judge.** I wrote B and C, so these picks come from the writer, not from a fresh reader. Session 2 is still a session that wrote nothing, and where its read differs from this one, trust it over me. I have tried to say plainly where the current chapter is better than either draft.

**Versions.** A = the chapter on main (`manuscript/chapters/Chapter__2_TheMiner.md`, 76 paragraphs). B = pace draft (67). C = dinner-table draft (67). B and C share paragraph numbers. A's numbers are different and are given beside each row.

## The short answer

- **No single version wins.** B is the best base for the argument sections (the columns' numbers, the terms, distance, the objections, the second cycle). A is better in the places where the chapter is looking at something: the porches, Bailey, the emptied towns, the miners at the end. C is the clearest where the reader meets a mechanism for the first time (how black lung works, how a bankruptcy moves a debt, what scrip was).
- **Of the six things I omitted on my own reading, I would restore one** (the sentence that the miners "had agency, and they used it"). Of the cuts the map called for, I would restore two of A's one-line turns.
- **Of the four additions, I would keep three and shorten one** (the scrip passage: keep the first paragraph, cut the second).
