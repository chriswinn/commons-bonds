## 3. Strongest-critic read of Draft B (run once)

Where a hostile but fair reader would stop. Questions for the author; nothing here was applied.

1. **Is the habitat rigged?** An economist will say the answer is "obvious" only because substitutes were ruled out by assumption, and that a real habitat might sensibly sell some ice to pay for finding more. Section 3 answers by loosening each condition. Is that enough, or should her refusal say she would sell if any of the four things were different?
2. **"The same ten thousand people."** In the rare-earths case the miner breathes more dust than the accountant. A reader who has just read Libby will notice. Should the passage say that inside the habitat the cost is unevenly shared but nobody can be kept from seeing it, or is that a step too many?
3. **Is 2008 the right case for social distance?** The paragraph asserts in two clauses that banks and families were not equally heard. Ch 1 has a nearer case that is yours: the colleague who could not ask for a raise. Use him here, keep 2008, or both?
4. **Ch 5 promises six covers and this chapter gives four kinds.** Ch 5 on main says "Chapter 7 names the full set of covers" and lists distance, time, a gap in knowledge, a gap in who can leave, routine and abundance. A reader counting will find four kinds, two habits and force. Change the Ch 5 sentence, or add a sentence here that maps the six onto the four?
5. **Force.** Section 6 says force is not a kind of distance, and section 9 shows it at Giza and the Qin wall. A critic will ask whether the book's main tool, pricing what distance hides, has anything to say where nothing is hidden. The draft answers only by declining to price forced labor. Is that the answer you want here, with the rest left to the chapters that treat it?
6. **The colony has no numbers.** "Estimate the rescue and the medical care, and divide by a thousand" is the whole demonstration of the book's new instrument. The TA has invented figures ready. Would four small numbers make the reserve real, or slow the section?
7. **What left the chapter.** The exoplanet case, the summary table and the roll call of later systems are gone, and the certification passage waits for Ch 9. Do you want any of them back? The table is the one a skimming reader would miss.
