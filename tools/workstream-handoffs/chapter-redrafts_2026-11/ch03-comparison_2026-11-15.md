- **"In the same year"** (A ¶83; B and C ¶77). The sources on file date the celebration of Maryland's five sanctuaries to August 2025 and the federal disaster request to February 2026. That is six months apart, not the same year. Suggested wording: "Six months after the program was celebrated…"

The citation spec gives Eskridge's quotation as "…(a) way of life — the whole package." So the words are his and should go back, inside the quotation, after one check against the article.
