## The three weakest passages in Pace (what you would still fix)

   Bismarck, and "the architecture is a choice." Break it at "None of this says."
2. **Paragraph 18:** "So once its investment returns are counted, the fund holds about as much as the oil's whole
   rent, and it holds it in permanent trust for the people the seabed belongs to and for the Norwegians who come
   after them." Two "holds" and two "it"s; I had to reread. Dinner's version is the fix.
3. **Paragraph 25:** "Every word of the objection is true, and it is the argument." It is not clear what "it"

cannot adopt by copying" (50) is awkward.
