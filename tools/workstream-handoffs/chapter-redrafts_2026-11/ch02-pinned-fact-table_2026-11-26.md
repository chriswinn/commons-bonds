# Chapter 2, The Miner: pinned fact table (started 2026-11-26)

**Status:** internal; a working table, not complete. Step 3 of the sources-first plan (harvest; request and
fetch; **pin**; draft from the table only; light claims check; the author's redline).

**The rule for drafting from this.** A sentence of fact goes into the next draft only if its row is in Part 1,
and it says no more than the words in the last column. Part 2 lists where Part 1 differs from the November 14
drafts. Part 3 lists what is not pinned yet and what each row waits on; nothing in Part 3 is to be drafted as
fact, and nothing in it is to be cut for want of a source until its search is recorded as exhausted.

**Built from** the claims check of the pace draft (`tools/audits/claims-check_ch02-pace-draft_2026-11-15.md`,
79 rows; "row" below is its number), the scrip file, and what the sources stage read on 2026-11-24 to 26.
Web sources have no page numbers; the "where" column gives the place in the document. Request numbers refer
to `tools/audits/library-requests_by-chapter-and-priority_2026-11-26.pdf`.

**Limit.** Rows marked *(as recorded)* carry the source's words as an earlier session wrote them down; this
session did not reopen those pages. Before the chapter is promoted, each is opened once more.
