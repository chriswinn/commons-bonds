## Questions to answer, per piece

1. **What it argues,** in two sentences, and which chapters it draws on.
2. **How much of the book it gives away.** Overlap with the chapters and proposal; whether it spends a chapter's
   best case or scene; whether that helps (platform, proof of audience) or costs (a publisher sees it already
   printed). Check the venue's **rights terms** (first serial rights, exclusivity, reprint into a book) against
   `rights-register.md` and the venue's current published guidelines.
3. **Venue fit and current terms.** Verify each target on the venue's own current submission page (open or
   closed, windows, simultaneous-submission policy, length). Say when a page could not be reached.
4. **Overlap between pieces.** Two pieces spending the same case; two pieces to the same outlet (e.g., The
   Atlantic main and Atlantic Ideas).
5. **Recommendation:** keep, redirect to another venue, merge, or retire.
