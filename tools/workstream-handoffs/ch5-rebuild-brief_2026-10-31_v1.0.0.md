## 1. What the chapter is, and the spine to preserve

Chapter 5 turns a scattered set of cases into **one mechanism** and then names the two-instrument
response. The structural claim: value is drawn from a shared, unproduced thing; the cost is real;
the cost is **severed** from the party that gained and fastened onto a party that cannot refuse; by
the time it comes due the beneficiary has gone. No villain — distance does the work.

**The climb (keep the order and the register-change at 2008):**
1. **Libby, Montana** — a town (mechanism small enough to hold in one hand).
2. **Deepwater Horizon** — an ecosystem / the dimensions of severance.
3. **Exxon Valdez** — brief pass-through (full decomposition is Ch 6).
4. **Baotou** — a global supply chain; the no-villain beat; substitutability limit.
5. **2008** — a national economy; **the hinge** (takes away the reader's alibi); the double transfer + four objections answered.
6. **Social Security** — time itself; relocates the severance to obligation-generation; rejects the $108T figure.
7. **The healthcare commons** — a single dying body (Butler); no-villain made concrete.

**Then the argument sections (keep all four, in order):** *The pattern made visible* · *The same
object, named honestly* (Polanyi/Fraser — the load-bearing passage) · *Who shaped the system?*
(Public Choice / rent-seeking complement) · *Restitution and foreclosure* (names the two instruments
+ lineage).

**Non-regression list (must survive the rebuild):** the no-villain reading; the Pistor *Code of
Capital* diagnosis at Libby; the Mexico-vs-US settlement contrast (legal architecture, not chemistry);
the Sweden 1930s counterfactual (rescue *and* relief is a choice); the honest-evidence posture at
Baotou (cite Zhang 2022, Zhou & Ge 2021; set aside the unsourceable ~$100B cleanup figure); the
four 2008 objections answered at full strength (agency, global, TARP-repaid, Dodd-Frank); the two
