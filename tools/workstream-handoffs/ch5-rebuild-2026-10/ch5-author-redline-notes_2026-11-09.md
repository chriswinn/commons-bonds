## What carries into the redraft

- **Opening: show, then name.** Open on a concrete scene and state the pattern plainly *after* the
  reader has something to hold (Ch 1's model: "The first thing he did in any new city was find the
  highest place in it and climb."). Give each candidate a **different** opening so the opening is
  tested too. A opens in Butler's father's room; the new B opens on its own spine's first scene.
- **One-pass readability** under the revised punctuation rule (pending the rule rewrite).
- **No repeated image within a page;** concrete verbs over neutral ones; hedges only where accuracy
  needs them.
- **"did not produce"** gets fixed once the author confirms what the "?" meant.
- **"Scatter" is retired** as a word and as a structure.
- **Mark 5 caution:** keep the verb concrete without implying bad faith (e.g. "cashed out"), so it
  doesn't cut against the chapter's no-villain reading. Author's call.
