**The chapter's question.** What did a ton of McDowell coal actually cost, and who should have been sitting in the empty chair? Opened in Canton ("He had the wrong defendant"). Answered column by column, then named, then shown running a second time on the same county.

**Book questions advanced.** "Who is left holding the bill?" gets four answers: a miner's lungs, a watershed, a town, the children who left. "Is this the full accounting?" is left open on purpose by the fifth column.

**Terms handed over.** "Cost severance," "severed cost" and "value extraction," after the reader has seen four columns. "Spatial cost severance" a section later.

**Opening person and place.** A senator driving the hollows of McDowell County in May 1960. One earned return: the same senator's indictment in Canton, with the defendant changed.

**Open structure calls for the author.**
1. **Three objections in a row.** *Recommend:* keep "no villain" whole, right after the word is named, because it is the book's spine. Cut "the coal was worth it" to three sentences. Move "the miners fought" (the 1968 strike, the Black Lung Association) up into the body column beside Bailey's testimony, where it is history with people in it and not a rebuttal.
2. **Does the senator get his name?** He is unnamed throughout. *Recommend:* name him once, at the podium in Canton. A reader thirteen years out, or reading in a second language, may not place a "young senator" in West Virginia in 1960.
3. **The lineage in section 9.** Two paragraphs credit Mazzucato, Harvey and Pigou in the middle of the naming. *Recommend:* one sentence each at the point of use, and keep the line that calling a man's lungs an externality files the worst thing in the chapter under a clerk's heading.
4. **The two enhancement candidates from June.** *Recommend:* cut both. The numbers section is stronger bare, and section 13 already carries the bridge to the bay.
5. **A company-store passage.** The TA decisions leave room for a sourced passage on coal-camp scrip here, if you want one. *Recommend:* not now. It would add a sixth column to a chapter built on five, and it needs a source read in full first. If you do want it, it belongs in the town column.
