**The chapter's question.** A small fishing nation found one of the largest oil fields on earth and did not spend it. Was that luck and temperament, or a design that can be built somewhere else? Opened at the fork ("settled by nothing in the rock"). Answered three times: Norway built it, Nigeria got the opposite from the same oil, and four places that look nothing like Norway built their own.

**Book questions advanced.** "Who is left holding the bill?" In Norway, far fewer people than anywhere else; in the Delta, everyone who lives there. "Is this the full accounting?" No: the four gaps at the end say what even Norway left unpriced.

**Terms handed over.** "Accountability bond" and "residual commons value," each in a sentence, with the building left to later chapters. The idea that a reserve makes a loss less final.

**Opening person and place.** A drilling crew on Block 2/4 in the North Sea, three days before Christmas 1969. One earned second scene: the hanging of Ken Saro-Wiwa and the Ogoni Nine. Earned return: the same drill at the close.
