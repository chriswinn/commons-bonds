# Everything still to find: Ch 1, Ch 5 and the Technical Appendix (master list)

**Status:** internal; started 2026-11-26 on the author's instruction.

**The rule he set (2026-11-26), in his words:** "I'd like to go ahead and find everything and then make
decisions once we have everything vs. make decisions to cut things instead of searching for them as that
potentially diminishes the impact, strength & value of this book at the cost of simply finding a source."
So no item below is skipped because its sentence might be cut. The "skip if" notes in
`tools/back-matter/library-list.md` no longer apply. Decisions come after the search.

**Built from** the three claims-check reports' own lists (sections "wrong or exposed," "wording," "nothing on
file," "not checked," and the appendix's source review), the library list, and the running list. Where a
report's full table has a row these summaries missed, add it here.

**Legend.** *Pin* = the fact is probably true and needs a citable source. *Read* = a named work nobody has
opened. *Origin* = a figure whose source has never been found.

## How this list is worked

1. A session takes Part B in order of what rides on each item, records the words and the page in
   `research/citations/`, and marks the row here.
2. What a session cannot reach after every route moves to Part A with what was tried.
3. Nothing is cut for want of a source until the search for it is recorded here as exhausted.
