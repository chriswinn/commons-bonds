### §3.1 Why is this intervention necessary? — the essay in the literature (load-bearing beat)

The essay assembles five scholarly traditions into a coherent bidirectional architecture. Each positioning verb is anchored to a concrete pivot the essay actually makes — name-dropping without substantive engagement would under-credit the essay's move.

| Tradition / book | Positioning verb | What the essay actually does |
|---|---|---|
| **Pistor, *Code of Capital* (2019)** — six legal modules (property; collateral; trust; corporate veil; bankruptcy; choice-of-law) | **EXTEND** | Pistor diagnoses (capital is a legal artifact). The essay extends from diagnostic to constructive: the two bond instruments are what Pistor's diagnostic implies about an honest accounting's missing tools. The Libby acquittal stops looking like a failure of prosecution and starts looking like a feature of the code (essay §III). |
| **Public Choice tradition (Buchanan + Tullock + Mueller)** | **PARALLEL (complement)** | Public Choice analyzes WHO maintains cost severance (rent-seeking; regulatory capture); the essay PRICES what the rent-seeking buys. Explicitly complementary, not contradiction — the essay says this in §III. |
| **Reparations economics (Coates → Darity-Mullen → Hamilton → Conley)** | **ADD TO + EXTEND-INTO-SETTING** | Restitution Bond positioned WITHIN Darity-Mullen's three-component typology (acknowledgment-and-atonement; redress decomposing into symbolic-redress + material-restitution; closure) — operationalizes the material-restitution component, AND extends the operational logic to the broader case-class of cost-severance harms (Libby asbestos; black lung; McDowell County; Niger Delta to Baotou). The framework JOINS the tradition; it does not rename or rebrand it (essay §V). |
| **Hartwick (1977) + Norway GPFG + SMCRA reclamation bonds + oil-and-gas decommissioning bonds** | **EXTEND** | Hartwick's intergenerational-equity rule + Norway's working sovereign fund + the SMCRA bond family are the existing instruments. The Foreclosure Bond extends the principle from one-resource extraction (mining; oil-and-gas) to multi-resource forward-looking obligation (climate; Social Security demographic shortfall) (essay §VI). |
| **Civic-republican tradition (Pettit + Skinner + Parfit)** | **PARALLEL (normative grounding)** | Republican non-domination + intergenerational impersonal-outcomes supply the philosophical grounding for the Foreclosure Bond's reach across time. The essay applies the tradition's commitments at intergenerational scope (essay §VI). |
| **Christophers, *The Price is Wrong* (2024)** | **PARALLEL (adjacent diagnostic)** | Christophers names how legal/financial architecture produces prices; the essay names what the produced prices fail to internalize. Single-sentence cross-reference in essay §III. |

**Anchoring case.** Read through this synthesis, Libby's 40-to-1 cost-to-revenue ratio + the 2009 acquittal of W. R. Grace and seven named executives stops being an anomaly of one bad company in one isolated valley. It is the visible shape of a legal architecture engineered to internalize the value side of extraction more reliably than the cost side. The essay names that mechanism (cost severance), identifies the two-direction shape of an honest accounting (backward = restitution; forward = foreclosure-in-the-older-sense, barring the future from options being closed off now), and assembles the bond instruments those directions would require from the legal-architectural moves the existing law already uses (reclamation bonds; decommissioning bonds; the coal-excise-tax-financed Black Lung Benefits Program; Norway GPFG).

### §3.2 Why now? — convergent timeliness signals (compressed; supports §3.1)

Three signals make the synthesis timely:

(a) **Pistor's *Code of Capital* (2019)** is central to law-and-political-economy scholarship and a Public Books-engaged touchstone (Pasquale's 2020 review).
(b) **Reparations economics has moved from advocacy register to operational-policy register** (Darity-Mullen 2020's ~$14T cumulative deficit; California Reparations Task Force 2023 making the methodology operational; HR 40 reintroduced).
(c) **Norway GPFG crossed $2 trillion in 2025-2026** — the working empirical counterfactual to "you can't actually build a Foreclosure Bond at scale."

The synthesis between these is overdue and the essay does it at a length suited to Public Books' published essay norms.

### §3.3 Why are you the right person?

The author has spent ~5 years building *Commons Bonds*, a forthcoming book that develops a formal framework for pricing the intergenerational costs that resource extraction systematically severs from the populations that bear them. The book is content-complete (10 chapters + Technical Appendix); the Pistor / Darity-Mullen / Hartwick synthesis isn't a fresh take written for the pitch — it is the book's bidirectional architecture being shown at essay scale.

Cross-sectoral career: National Institutes of Health during the Human Genome Project; federal research and consulting; CEO of a healthcare-IT firm; now a nursing student at Virginia Peninsula Community College. The pattern recognition behind the cost-severance frame draws on having watched the mechanism operate from inside federal-research, healthcare-CEO, and healthcare-worker-training seats successively.

Discipline anchors the pitch can signal credibly: (i) public-record-strict sourcing (the essay's named-subject inventory is on-record-public-utterance subjects only; living-private subjects pursued through anonymization or consent collection); (ii) named-tradition-respecting attribution (Restitution Bond explicitly positioned within Darity-Mullen's typology; Foreclosure Bond explicitly positioned within Hartwick-and-Norway's lineage and Pettit-Skinner's republican grounding); (iii) proactive AI-collaboration disclosure with documented Open Insights Log.

## §4. Hook — the load-bearing first-paragraph decision

The hook decision is the single most consequential one in this brief. The Public Books editorial-team-mail-triage scan reads the first 2-3 sentences cold. A weak hook loses the rest.

Four candidate hooks considered:

| Hook | Substance | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Vermiculite-and-ratio** | "For every dollar W. R. Grace's vermiculite mine outside Libby, Montana, earned across twenty-seven years, the surrounding world absorbed forty in damage. The 2009 acquittal of the company and seven named executives — after the largest environmental criminal prosecution in American history — was not a failure of evidence. The legal architecture available to the prosecution was not built to reach a harm assembled out of thousands of small decisions spread across thirty years." | **RECOMMENDED.** Most arresting empirical anchor in the essay; lands the 40-to-1 + the acquittal + the structural-mechanism pivot in three sentences. Editor sees concrete-case + counterintuitive-result + framework-payoff signal. Mirrors the essay's own opening. |
| B — Naming-the-mechanism | "Cost severance — the structural separation of the party that captures the value of an activity from the party who absorbs its costs — recurs across Libby's vermiculite mine, Deepwater Horizon, the 2008 foreclosure cascade, and the Social Security obligation maturing in the 2030s." | Conceptual-first. Stronger after the hook; weaker as the hook. Triage reader needs the case-anchor first. |
| C — Two-direction | "The accountability gap has two directions. Some of the harm has already been realized; the question is restitution. Some has not yet been realized but is in motion; the question is foreclosure in the older sense of the word — barring a future party from options being closed off in the present." | Architectural-first. Good editorial-paragraph but pre-empts the essay's payoff before establishing why the gap exists. |
| D — Pistor-lineage | "Katharina Pistor's *Code of Capital* showed that capital is a legal artifact — that an ordinary asset becomes capital only when the law endows it with property, collateral, trust, the corporate veil, bankruptcy, and choice-of-law..." | Bookshelf-first. Public Books-resonant register but leads with another author's name rather than the essay's own move. Better as §III content than as hook. |

**LOCKED: Hook A (Vermiculite-and-ratio).** The 40-to-1 ratio + largest-prosecution acquittal is the essay's most arresting single anchor; the structural-mechanism pivot in the third sentence flips the surface reading and signals the framework payoff without spending words on the framework itself.

## §5. Voice register — locked

- **Academic-public, intellectually-engaged.** Names scholarly traditions explicitly (Pistor; Darity-Mullen; Hamilton; Coates; Conley; Hartwick; Pettit; Skinner; Parfit; Christophers — the essay's lineage; the pitch points at the engagement without exhaustively listing).
- **Direct, plain prose.** No throat-clearing meta-commentary. No "I would argue that..." hedge. The argument is stated.
- **No civic-republican / institutional-measurement framing in the BR-coded register** — that lands BR, not Public Books. Reframe as "academic-public-intellectual lane" register: scholarly engagement, lineage attribution, synthesis across traditions. The Pasquale "Tales from the Crypto" review is the closest tonal reference.
- **No em-dashes used as voice crutch.** Em-dashes for parenthetical insertions where they're load-bearing; not as connective tissue. The essay itself ships at 0 em-dashes (Amendment E new-pipeline) — the pitch should match.
- **Lineage attribution discipline.** Restitution Bond positioned *within* Darity-Mullen's three-component typology (not alongside it; not as a rebrand). Foreclosure Bond explicitly grounds in Hartwick + Norway empirical + Pettit-Skinner-Parfit philosophical lineage. The pitch carries enough of this for the editor to see the discipline; the essay does the full work.
- **No first-person memoir.** The author's bio enters in §3.3 as credentialing, not as voice register for the body.
- **No mention of the Boston Review decline.** Same-day editorial-triage decline is information-poor signal; clean fresh-submission framing is editorially more confident. Cascade-positioning belongs in the literary-agent query later, not in the venue pitch.

## §10. Subject line — locked (recommendation pending author final confirmation)

**Recommended (Stage 1 lock): "PITCH: The Accountability Gap — what the Libby acquittal couldn't reach"**
