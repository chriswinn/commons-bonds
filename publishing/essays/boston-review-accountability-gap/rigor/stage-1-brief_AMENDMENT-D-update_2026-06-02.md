## §1. Artifact-class assignment (Amendment D §0.5.1)

**Artifact class:** **End-reader-facing essay/chapter/op-ed prose.**

**Specific subclass:** Long-form magazine essay (3,000–5,000w band) destined for Boston Review's Forum series or standalone-essay placement, derived from Ch 5 via the v3.1 audience-blind Stage 2 discipline.

**Reasoning:**
- The essay is the primary output object — the prose readers encounter at boston review or in the Forum-series respondent package.
- The reception chain is **direct**: editor → editorial board → magazine reader → respondent cluster (in Forum series) → cited-tradition successors who will read what landed in print. All of these are READERS of the essay prose itself, not consultants reading a query to decide whether to assign the essay or projected-downstream readers reading at one or more removes from the artifact.
- This contrasts with the BR cover letter (which is a single-direct-reader artifact — only the editor receives it — and is therefore under a distinct artifact-class with different reception-chain weighting; see §3 below for the scope-distinct framing).
- This also contrasts with the book proposal package (which is a different artifact class: agent-and-acquisitions-editor-facing query-and-platform document where the "reader" is in fact a gatekeeper-consultant deciding whether to take the project to a readership that will only encounter the prose after acquisition).

The Amendment D artifact-class taxonomy (per memory entry 2026-05-31 + Aeon Option E.2 γ.1 empirical anchor): the same prose move can be right at one artifact class and wrong at another. The Aeon empirical anchor was Option β mediation clause — right at essay-prose-scale, wrong at editor-pitch-scale. The principle is reception-chain calibration: read what the artifact does for its actual direct readers, not for projected downstream consultants who will only encounter it second-hand.

For this BR essay, the artifact class is decisively "end-reader-facing essay." Per the parent brief §4 (structural decisions) + §5 (voice register) + §11 (length budget), the essay is structured for direct readership at BR's masthead — institutional-measurement register, third-person throughout, ~4,500w body, civic-republican / democratic-theory grounding, plain prose without apparatus. All structural commitments confirm the artifact-class assignment.

## §3. Operational consequence at Stage 3 (Amendment D §0.5.3)

The reception-chain weighting changes how Pass 3.1 + 3.2 + 3.3 + 3.4 + 3.5 finding-severity aggregations read the per-character data. The per-character verdicts themselves are unchanged; the aggregate-verdict synthesis is reweighted.

### Pass 3.1 (fact-check) — Amendment D weighting

A factual error or precision miss that affects DIRECT readers' direct comprehension is higher-severity than a factual error or precision miss that affects only PROJECTED-DOWNSTREAM readers' citation-tracking verification.

- **HIGH-severity (direct-reader-affecting):** factual errors in the four principal cases (Libby, Deepwater, 2008, Social Security) that any of C-2 + C-11 + C-12 + C-13 + C-15 + C-16 would catch on read. Examples from V-E history: F-FCIC-FIX (pre-existing fabrication of FCIC verbatim quote) was HIGH-severity because C-2 + C-11 + C-12 are direct readers of the §II FCIC paragraph and any one of them could have caught the verbatim-fidelity miss against the actual FCIC report.
- **MEDIUM-severity (consultant-affecting):** factual errors that the editor-consultant (C-1) would flag in their projection-of-readership lens, or that the literary-agent / acquisitions-editor consultants (C-3 / C-4) would read as comp-titles-credibility signal degradation.
- **LOWER-severity (projected-downstream-affecting):** precision misses in tradition-citation chains that only specialist-tradition readers (C-5 through C-10, C-14) would catch in citation-tracking. Examples from V-E history: F-FRESH-3.1-HAMILTON publisher-attribution correction was MEDIUM-severity in the Amendment-D-aware aggregation because it primarily affects C-5 + C-10 (projected-downstream readers reading bibliography discipline signals).

### Pass 3.2 (voice-polish) — Amendment D weighting

Voice-register concerns at direct-reader-pace are higher-priority than concerns affecting only projected-downstream specialist register.

- **HIGH-severity (direct-reader-affecting):** expository flatness; meta-commentary; rule-of-three LLM tics; hedge phrases; sentence-length-variance failures that BR's masthead-reader (C-2) + general policy-curious reader (C-11) + first-gen reader (C-15) would experience as register-broken or LLM-prose-detectable on a single read.

- **LOWER-severity (projected-downstream-affecting):** specialist-register precision concerns that only tradition-cluster readers (C-5 through C-10) would notice on close re-read.
