**Where B is genuinely better, and what to harvest:** B foregrounds the lit-positioning more *visibly* and reads more overtly "confident-structural" — which is the Noema register dial. Its sharpest single move is the Polanyi/Fraser disagreement framed as *the contribution itself*: "here is where I break with the tradition I most belong to, openly, because the break is the contribution." A's Polanyi/Fraser turn ("Where I part from the lineage is on the cure") is correct but softer. The kickoff dial says **lean INTO the Polanyi/Fraser/Mazzucato critique** — so harvest B's confident framing into A. (This is framing energy, not a new epigram; it does not add to the antithesis budget.)

**Net:** the PRIMARY criterion (reconstructability) is *met by A*; B's edge there is prominence, which is secondary to the binding placement rule that B risks. A wins on both binding disciplines; the one thing B does better folds in cleanly.
