## 2. Structure

The map's nine sections. Notes:

- **Section 1 and 2 (the habitat).** This is the one place in the book where invented people and an invented place are allowed, because the reader is told they are imagined. v3's addendum asked for a fully rendered administrator scene; that stands. Keep her concrete (the request on her desk, the kindergarten two corridors away, the domes she walks past). Walk her reasoning in her terms, one consideration at a time, without the formula's symbols. **Discount direction guard (v3):** the future counts almost fully, so the discount rate is near zero and the discount factor is near one. Do not invert it.
- **Section 4 (other worlds).** Brisk. Each case is a paragraph that ends on its verdict. The Moon's two opposite answers is the teaching point. Europa is an Open entry: say that the cost can't be priced and that waiting is the answer, and don't apologize for it.
- **Section 6 (distance).** Each kind gets a case the reader has already met and one sentence on why it is not available in the habitat. Then the turn: seen from the habitat, the remarkable thing about Earth is how much it provides without being asked. Keep that passage short and concrete.
- **Section 7.** The ranking as a short table or a tight paragraph, not five paragraphs of chemistry. It hands the transition metals to Ch 9.
- **Section 8.** Two objections, answered once each. The frictionless-plane answer in a few sentences. The defense of thought experiments as method (v3 Beat 10) in two sentences inside it.
- **Section 9.** Giza and the Qin from the documentary record only, hedged as v3's correction flags require. No dollar figure for forced labor, ever.
- **Close.** Hand to Ch 8 with the 1960 price in words ("just under five dollars"). The current close uses the retired value.
