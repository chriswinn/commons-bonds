For harm already done I borrow your method of matched comparison, and I treat the result as a debt that is posted and kept on the books until it is paid.
   Where: Chapter 5, page 19 (the method "works in two directions") and page 26 (the matched comparisons for McDowell County, Libby and Appalachia). Appendix §5.5 (pages 30–33) and Definition 1.12 (page 18).
3. Does the name "reparations bond" fit your and Kirsten Mullen's program? One sentence in Chapter 5 says that, applied to the debt owed to the descendants of enslaved people in the United States, the Restitution Bond is a reparations bond, in the amount your method sets. If that name claims too much, or sits wrongly beside your program, I would rather cut it than keep it and offend someone I hold in the highest regards.
   Where: Chapter 5, page 26 ("Applied to that debt, the Restitution Bond is a reparations bond…"). The appendix does not use the phrase.
